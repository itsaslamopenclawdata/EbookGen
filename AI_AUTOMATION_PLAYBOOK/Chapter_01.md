## What "AI Automation" Actually Means (No Jargon)

Let me cut through the noise.

**AI automation** is using artificial intelligence tools to handle tasks that would otherwise require human time — without you being actively involved in every step.

It's not one super-intelligent robot. It's a system of specialized tools that each do one thing very well, connected together so they run without you micromanaging.

Think of it like this:

- **Traditional business:** You hire a virtual assistant, they do tasks, you review everything
- **AI-automated business:** You set up AI agents, they handle routine work, you only step in for decisions

The key word is **autonomous**. You set the direction. The AI handles the execution.
