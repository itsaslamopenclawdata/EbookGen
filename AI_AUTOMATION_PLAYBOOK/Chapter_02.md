## The Problem With Most "AI Stack" Articles

If you've ever Googled "best AI tools for business," you know the problem: hundreds of articles recommending 50+ tools, none of them explaining how the tools actually work together.

That's not what we're doing here.

After testing dozens of AI tools and automation platforms in real businesses, I've found that **5 categories of tools cover 90% of what you need**. Not 50 tools. Not an overwhelming list. Five categories, with one or two recommended options in each.

Master these. Connect them together. Your AI stack is done.

## The 5-Tool Core Stack

### Tool Category 1: Reasoning & Writing AI
**The brain of your operation**

This is where your main AI assistant lives. It handles:
- Writing content (emails, social posts, proposals)
- Reasoning through problems
- Generating ideas and strategies
- Drafting documents, scripts, and frameworks

**Your options:**

| Tool | Best For | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **Claude (Anthropic)** | Complex reasoning, long documents, nuanced writing | Free tier / $20/mo Pro |
| **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** | Versatile, fast, strong plugin ecosystem | Free tier / $20/mo Plus |
| **Gemini (Google)** | Integration with Google Workspace, research | Free tier / $20/mo Advanced |

**Recommendation:** Use Claude as your primary. It handles longer上下文 and produces more nuanced output. Keep ChatGPT as a backup for different perspectives.

**Setup time:** 10 minutes. Create accounts at claude.ai and chat.openai.com.

### Tool Category 2: AI Orchestration & Automation
**The conductor that connects everything**

This is the tool that ties your entire operation together. It runs AI agents that can:
- Research topics autonomously
- Execute multi-step tasks without you supervising
- Coordinate other tools and platforms
- Run on schedules or on-demand

**Your options:**

| Tool | Best For | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **OpenClaw** | Your personal AI agent team, orchestration, tool connections | Free / scales with usage |
| **Make.com (formerly Integromat)** | Visual automation workflows between apps | Free tier / $9–$49/mo |
| **n8n** | Self-hosted, developer-focused automation | Free (self-hosted) / $20/mo cloud |

**Recommendation:** Start with **OpenClaw** — it's built specifically for orchestrating AI agents and connects directly to your workflow. It handles the multi-agent coordination that other tools struggle with.

**Setup time:** 20 minutes for OpenClaw basic setup.

### Tool Category 3: Workflow Automation
**The glue that connects your apps**

These tools trigger actions based on events and move data between platforms:
- "When I get a new lead email, add them to my CRM and start an email sequence"
- "When someone buys my product, give them access and notify me"
- "When I publish a blog post, automatically share it to all my social accounts"

**Your options:**

| Tool | Best For | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **Zapier** | Easiest to use, 5,000+ app connections | Free tier / $19–$59/mo |
| **Make.com** | More powerful workflows, better logic | $9–$49/mo |
| **n8n** | Developer control, self-hosting option | Free (self-hosted) |

**Recommendation:** **Zapier** if you want the path of least resistance. **Make.com** if you want more power and are willing to learn a slightly more complex interface.

**Setup time:** 15 minutes to create your first automation.

These tools let AI agents interact with websites, extract data, and perform web-based tasks:
- Lead research (finding emails, company info, social profiles)
- Competitive analysis
- Data collection from any website
- Content aggregation

**Your options:**

| Tool | Best For | Cost |
|------|----------|------|

**Setup time:** 10 minutes.

## The Complete Decision Tree: Which Tool for Which Task

When you're unsure what to use, follow this:

```
Is this a task that requires AI reasoning, writing, or decisions?
├── YES → Use Claude or ChatGPT
└── NO → Move to next step

Is this a task that requires coordinating multiple AI agents?
├── YES → Use OpenClaw
└── NO → Move to next step

Is this a task that triggers action between two apps?
├── YES → Use Zapier or Make.com
└── NO → Move to next step

Is this a task that requires browsing the web or extracting data?
├── YES → Use OpenClaw Browser Tool
└── NO → Use Canva (if design) or skip
```

## What to Avoid

Three common mistakes that waste time and money:

**Mistake 1: Tool Hoarding**
Don't sign up for 30 different AI tools. Five categories, one tool per category, done.

**Mistake 2: Automation for Things That Change Constantly**
If a task changes every time (custom client work, complex negotiations), AI can't fully automate it. Save automation for repeatable, rule-based tasks.

**Mistake 3: Setting Everything Up Perfectly Before Starting**
You'll never feel ready. Set up the minimum viable stack (Chapter 3 will show you how to decide what to build first), launch it, then refine as you learn.
